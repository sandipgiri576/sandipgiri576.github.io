LEGO-based generation

Reusable blocks, controlled assembly

The strategy begins with chemically meaningful fragments rather than arbitrary atoms. Connection rules assemble them into candidate molecules or structures, property models screen the generated space, and quantum chemistry refines selected candidates. This makes the design logic traceable and adaptable across related chemical domains.

  1. RepresentDefine fragments and connection sites.
  2. GenerateExplore combinations under chemical rules.
  3. PrioritizeUse predicted properties and synthesizability.
  4. ValidateApply quantum and atomistic calculations.
